FAQ

Questions people ask before joining

Something else on your mind? A counsellor can answer it on a quick call.

Do I need to know coding?

No. Every track except "Build with AI" is fully no-code. "Build with AI" uses AI coding tools, so basic computer comfort is enough.

What if I miss a live class?

Recordings are available within 24 hours, and the Sunday build session is a good place to catch up with a mentor.

Which tools will I need to pay for?

None. We teach on the free tiers of every tool. Optional paid upgrades are explained, never required.

Is there a student discount or EMI?

Yes. Students with a valid college ID get a discount, and no-cost EMI is available at checkout.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. You get a full refund if you cancel before the second live class, no questions asked.
