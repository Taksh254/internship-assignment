My approach

The key decisions behind each part, in brief.

PawPoint

MVP + CTAs
  • One job for the MVP: find the right care for my animal, near me, right now. Accounts, payments and reviews come later.
  • Start from the pet and the problem, not the clinic type. A "Who's the patient?" picker and shortcuts like "Vaccination due" or "Ate something bad" lead straight to the right care.
  • Emergency is always one tap away, in the header, a banner and the phone's bottom bar. Red is used only for emergencies.
  • The main button changes by category: you book a vet, call an ambulance and report a rescue.
  • Trust is built in: phone-verified badges, a "checked 3 days ago" date on every listing, fees shown upfront, and Submit a Correction on every card to keep data fresh.
  • Supply side: List Your Business is free and phone-verified within 48 hours, with a separate flow to claim an existing listing.

Kaarya AI

Lead capture
  • Value proposition: learn AI by building real things, and leave with a portfolio of projects you can show a recruiter.
  • CTAs ordered by commitment: Register (high), Talk to a counsellor (medium) and Get the free syllabus (low), so every visitor has a next step.
  • Two-step registration with a partial lead saved after step 1, so the team can follow up with people who drop off.
  • Pay after a free orientation call, which lowers the barrier to registering. Every lead is tagged with the CTA it came from.
  • In production: forms post to an n8n webhook, which writes to Google Sheets or a CRM and sends WhatsApp and email confirmations.

Carousel system

AI workflow
  • The founder does two steps: drop a raw idea, then review. n8n runs the rest.
  • Claude researches and writes the slides as structured JSON with word limits per slide, so the text stays editable and one slide can be fixed without redoing the whole carousel.
  • Design comes from a brand template: Canva Bulk Create for editable designs, or the HTML renderer I built for fully automatic PNGs and a LinkedIn PDF.
  • A feedback loop: plain-English edits like "slide 4 is too long" go back to the AI, and weekly results feed the best hooks back into the prompt.
